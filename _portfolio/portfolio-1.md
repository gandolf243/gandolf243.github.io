BridgePatcher is an independent community project working to bring newer macOS releases and features to unsupported Macs. Its work includes graphics acceleration, Wi-Fi, audio, and T1/T2 support.

[View BridgePatcher on GitHub](https://github.com/bridgepatcher/BridgePatcher)
